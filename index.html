RivetBill Invoice Maker Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

This policy describes how RivetBill Invoice Maker for Android, developed by Zlabya, handles information.

Information stored on your device

The app uses the information you enter to create invoices, quotes and related documents, track payments, and generate reports. It stores your business profile, customer records, item catalog, documents and payment records in a local database in the app’s private storage. This may include names, contact details, addresses, tax identifiers, descriptions, amounts, dates and notes. The app records payment details you enter and does not process card payments.

If you select a logo from your photo library or draw a signature, the app saves a copy in its private storage for use on documents. The app has no sign-in or developer-run cloud sync and does not send your invoice records to Zlabya.

Advertising and privacy choices

Android versions with advertising enabled show a banner provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, which can indicate approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID. The SDK encrypts this data in transit. RivetBill does not pass your invoice, customer, or payment records to AdMob.

Where a consent prompt is required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present it before requesting ads. You can revisit available choices in the app under Settings, then Privacy, then Ad privacy choices. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Sharing, printing and Android services

The app generates document PDFs and CSV reports on your device. If you choose to share, export or print, Android sends the selected content to the app, service or printer you select. Payment reminders may include customer and invoice details, the balance due and an attached invoice PDF. The recipients and services you choose handle their own copies.

Android may back up or transfer app data based on your device and backup settings. The app can also ask Google Play to display its review prompt. Google Play handles reviews under Google’s privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

App records are kept on your device until you remove them or clear the app’s stored data. You can delete draft documents, customer records, catalog items and payment entries using the app’s controls. Issued documents remain in the local history and may keep billing details even after you delete the matching customer record. To remove all local app records, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings. Manage Android backups, saved exports and copies already shared separately.
